Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Is QELVO free?

Yes. Everything is free during the beta.

Do I need to download anything?

No. QELVO runs in your browser. For the best experience on iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it opens full screen like any other app. On Android, use Install app from the browser menu.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add subscriptions and expenses yourself, which keeps your bank details completely out of it.

Do my friends need QELVO too?

Only if they want to see the group live on their own phone. They open your invite link and sign in with their email. No password needed.

Which currencies does it support?

US dollars, Canadian dollars and UAE dirhams. Each subscription keeps its own currency, and your total converts to the one you choose.
